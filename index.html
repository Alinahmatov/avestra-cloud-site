What it sees

A room, a database, a journal.

Local AI on Windows. It recognizes faces, names objects, writes what people did, counts who is in frame, and will not open without a license. No one else’s cloud.

Face recognition

Enroll faces on this PC. Known people are named. Everyone else stays Unknown until you put them in the database. It names itself — you don’t type labels on every frame.

Object detection

The scene is not empty space. Chair. Laptop. Phone. Bed. Everyday objects get names so the room is readable — and triggers can fire on what is actually there.

Action journal

Who entered. Who left. Wave, sit, stand, possible fall. The journal is the log of what happened in your space — searchable, not a vibe, not a slideshow.

People database

Its own local store. Enrolled faces, unnamed tracks, history. The database lives next to the app, not in a vendor account you don’t control.

Occupancy

How many people are in frame right now. Peak and unique counts for the day. Watch your space without guessing from a silent thumbnail.

How it works

Install it. Point it. Read who walked through.

  1. 1

    Install on Windows

    Download AvestraCloud-Setup.exe, run it, then sign in. The camera and models stay on this PC. Installing does not create an account.

  2. 2

    Fill the people database

    Add faces for the people you want named. Anyone else stays Unknown — still tracked, still journaled.

  3. 3

    Decide what matters

    Unknown walked through. A known person waved. Capture, log, email — you write the rules.

  4. 4

    Start watching

    Hit Start. Live view, occupancy, action journal. The PC stays on. The cloud is this machine.

On this PC

The computer is the cloud.

Footage stays here

Video, embeddings, the people database, the journal — on the machine that runs Avestra Cloud. There is no Avestra-hosted video pipe. Watch your space without shipping it out.

Estimates are estimates

Age and height, when shown, are camera guesses with confidence — not papers, not facts. Treat them as hints. Never as identity.

You name who it names

Recognition only speaks names you enrolled. Triggers only do what you configure. Optional models stay dark until you turn them on.

You are responsible for using the software in line with local law, workplace policy, and consent requirements for camera and biometric monitoring.
